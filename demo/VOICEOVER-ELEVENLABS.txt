If you use an Android phone with a Mac, you probably know the problem. iPhone users get their texts, calls and notifications right on their Mac. They copy on one device and paste on the other, and send files in a second. But with Android, the phone and the Mac basically ignore each other. So you keep picking up your phone, emailing files to yourself, and typing login codes by hand.

So I built PhoneBridge. It's a free, open-source app that connects your Android phone to your Mac over your own Wi-Fi. Notifications, messages, calls, files, your clipboard and more, all on your Mac, with no cloud and no account.

They find each other in a few seconds, and both screens show the same six-digit code. I confirm it once, and they're paired. There's no account to sign up for, no cloud, and no PhoneBridge servers. Everything stays between these two devices, and it's encrypted.
