# PhoneBridge demo: voice-over script

Written for `PhoneBridge Demo.mov` (12:22, no voice in the recording). Timecodes are from the raw file.
Gaps between rows are dead air you can trim. A short 75-second cut is at the end.

Pace: about 2.5 words a second. Each line fits its slot with room to breathe.

---

## Full walkthrough (~12 min)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|------|-----------|------------|
| 0:00–0:12 | Mac: "Looking for nearby phones". Phone: "Looking for your Mac". | This is PhoneBridge. It puts my Android phone inside my Mac. When I open it, the Mac and the phone look for each other on the same Wi-Fi. |
| 0:12–0:18 | Both show the same pairing code. | They show the same six-digit code. I confirm it once, and they're paired. No account, no cloud. Everything stays between these two devices, encrypted. |
| 0:18–0:45 | Mac window: phone name, battery, now playing, shortcuts. Phone: the "Your Mac" panel. | On the Mac, I see my phone's battery, what's playing, and one-tap actions. And on the phone there's a matching panel for the Mac: lock it, sleep it, control its music and volume. |
| 0:48–1:00 | Picture on the Mac is sent with "Send file" and opens on the phone. | Sending a file is one click. Here's a picture on my Mac. I send it, and it's already open on the phone. |
| 1:06–1:36 | On the phone: Google Images, a photo shared to the Mac, it lands in Downloads. | It works the other way too. I find a photo on my phone, share it to PhoneBridge, and it's in my Mac's Downloads folder. |
| 1:42–1:54 | Mac Settings: lock when phone walks away, phone apps, phone as microphone, screenshots. | A quick look at settings. The Mac can lock itself when my phone walks away. I can use the phone as a microphone, and Mac screenshots can go straight to the phone. |

Whatever I type on the Mac shows up on the phone as I type. Here's my to-do list. And it works the other way too: I type on the phone, and it's on the Mac. |
| 4:12–4:54 | Device & Find My Phone: battery, flashlight, Do Not Disturb, ring, vibrate, map. Opened in Maps. | The Device page shows battery and charging. From here I can turn on the flashlight, switch on Do Not Disturb, ring or vibrate the phone, and see where it is on a map. One click opens it in Maps. |
| 4:54–5:18 | Photos & Videos list, then Notification History. | Every photo and video on the phone, and every notification it's had, all searchable from the Mac. |
| 5:24–6:36 | Safari tabs (YouTube, LinkedIn, GitHub) open on the phone with "Mac's tab". | Watching something on the Mac and need to leave? I tap "Mac's tab" on the phone, and the page I was on opens right there. YouTube, LinkedIn, even the PhoneBridge GitHub page. |
| 6:42–7:42 | Phone: copy news text, it shows up in Mac Notes. Share a news image, it opens on the Mac. | The clipboard is shared. I copy a headline on the phone and paste it straight into Notes on the Mac. Same with an image: share it, and it opens on the Mac. |
| 7:48–8:18 | Copy text on GitHub on the Mac, paste it on the phone. | And Mac to phone: I copy text in Safari and paste it on the phone. No emailing myself, no cables. |
| 8:24–8:42 | Phone shows "Now playing on Mac". Pause, play, next. | Music on the Mac? The phone becomes the remote: play, pause, skip, volume. |
| 8:48–9:48 | Phone: Trackpad mode. Cursor moves around Finder. | My phone can also be a trackpad. One finger moves, tap to click, two fingers scroll. |
| 9:48–10:30 | Phone: Keyboard mode. Typing, with emoji, into Mac Notes. | And a keyboard. I type on my phone, and it shows up in Notes on the Mac, emoji included. |
| 10:30–10:54 | Mac Shortcuts. "Find Mac" plays a sound on the Mac. | I can run any Mac shortcut from the phone. And if I've lost my Mac somewhere at home, Find Mac plays a sound on it. |
| 10:54–11:48 | Scan to Mac: camera, handwritten page, filters, Next. The scan opens on the Mac. | My favourite: Scan to Mac. I point the phone at my handwritten notes, clean them up, tap Next, and the scan is waiting on my Mac. |
| 11:48–12:22 | Back to the home panels on both devices. | That's PhoneBridge. Your Android phone, built into your Mac. It's free and open source, and the link is below. |

## Before you record the voice

- Some views blur private content (Messages, Calls, Photos & Videos). Pause on those frames before posting and make sure nothing personal shows.
- The device names on screen ("Abhinav's M36", "Abhinav's MacBook Air") stay visible. Rename them in Settings and re-record if you don't want that.
